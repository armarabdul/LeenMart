PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT
Leen Mart
1. Executive Summary
Leen Mart is a multi-vendor marketplace platform designed to support a wide range of
sellers—from individual vendors to large businesses—focused initially on a regional market,
with scalability to pan-India.
The platform differentiates itself through:
● Flexible vendor monetization (commission OR subscription)
● Strong preorder system for time-sensitive goods (food, fish, etc.)
● Hybrid fulfillment model (pickup + vendor-managed delivery)
● Trust-first approach (manual approvals + fraud controls)
2. Objectives
Primary Goals
● Enable vendors to easily set up and manage online shops
● Provide customers with a seamless mobile-first buying experience
● Ensure platform trust via approval and fraud detection systems
Secondary Goals
● Support hyperlocal commerce
● Enable preorders for time-sensitive inventory
● Scale to national-level operations
3. User Roles
3.1 Customer
● Browse products
● Place orders (delivery or pickup)
● Make payments (online or COD if available)
● Leave reviews
● Report fraud
3.2 Vendor
● Register and complete KYC
● Create and manage shop
● Add products (subject to approval)
● Configure delivery/pickup
● Manage orders and preorders
3.3 Admin
● Approve/reject products
● Monitor fraud
● Manage vendors and users
● Handle disputes (non-mediator role)
● Analytics and reporting
4. Business Model
4.1 Revenue Streams
1. Commission-Based
○ Category-based commission (e.g., food, electronics, vehicles)
2. Subscription-Based
○ Tiered plans:
■ Basic (limited products)
■ Pro (medium scale)
■ Enterprise (large catalog)
5. Core Features
5.1 Vendor Onboarding
● Vendor registration
● Mandatory KYC verification
● Shop profile creation
5.2 Product Management
● Vendors can add products
● Products require manual admin approval
● Support for:
○ New products
○ Second-hand goods
○ Services (optional future)
5.3 Preorder System (Key Feature)
Capabilities:
● Vendors define:
○ Quantity limit
○ Preorder start time
○ Expiry date/time
○ Advance payment (% configurable)
Example Use Cases:
● Cake orders for next day
● Fish sales for morning pickup
● Limited stock items
Behavior:
● Once quantity exceeded → Out of Stock
● After expiry → Listing disabled
5.4 Order Management
Order Types:
1. Delivery
2. Pickup
5.5 Delivery System
● Platform does NOT provide delivery
● Vendor controls:
○ Delivery radius
○ Charges
○ Time slots
Customer Flow:
● Select delivery
● Enter exact location
● Choose time slot
5.6 Pickup System (QR-Based)
● Customer selects pickup time
● System generates secure QR code
● Vendor scans QR → order marked complete
Rules:
● QR valid until scanned
● Prevents fraud and misuse
5.7 Payment System
Integration:
● Razorpay
Payment Types:
● Online payment
● Cash on Delivery (restricted)
Rules:
● Payments go to platform first
● Settlement to vendors later
● COD only for trusted vendors
5.8 Reviews & Ratings
Supported:
● Product reviews
● Shop reviews
Features:
● Rating system (1–5)
● Text feedback
● Abuse reporting
5.9 Notifications
● Email
● SMS
● Push notifications (PWA)
Events:
● Order confirmation
● Payment success/failure
● Delivery/pickup reminders
● Preorder alerts
5.10 Fraud & Safety System
Features:
● Rule-based fraud detection
● Transaction hold capability
● User reporting system
Examples:
● Multiple complaints → auto-flag vendor
● Suspicious transactions → hold funds
