# LEEN MART — BUSINESS DECISION REGISTER

**Document ID:** LM-DEC-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 22 August 2026
**Source:** Derived from `01-requirements-gap-analysis.md`, `02-leen-mart-sdd.md`, and a direct code/schema audit (Business Decision + Architecture Freeze audit, August 2026).
**Purpose:** A single, trackable checklist of every unresolved P0/P1 business decision blocking further Stage 3+ implementation, for the business owner to approve, reject, or amend. This document **decides nothing itself** — every open row is a question, not a specification.

**How to use this register:** For each decision below, fill in the **Approval / Status** field and return it. Anything marked **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED** cannot be resolved by engineering, product judgement, or precedent from other marketplaces — it needs a named owner's explicit sign-off, and in several cases a CA or legal opinion in addition to a business owner's.

**What this document is not:** It is not an implementation plan, not an API spec, not a schema design, and it invents no numbers. Every rate, window, threshold, and rule that does not already exist in the repository is left blank and flagged, on purpose.
